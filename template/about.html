About the application


What is this application?

This application gives predictive guidance of the next-day bitcoin price volatility. The aim of this application is to guide BTC investor decision-making processes through the result obtained by the prediction model. This application integrates predictive machine-learning volatility modelling alongside with a comprehensive LLM-based explanation.


How does this application work?

For the prediction model, I used Random Forest Regression which is suitable for non-linear data points (since volatility must not be linear from time to time), supported by GARCH time series model to capture the dynamics of time-to-time volatility (see my GitHub repo (/model directory) for more details about the modelling).


The explanation is generated by the LLM, particularly by OpenAI API calls. The explanation is generated every four hours scheduled by APScheduler. The external market context utilizes CoinGecko for Bitcoin market information, FRED for general market information (including interest rates and market volatility index (VIX)), and AlphaVantage for relevant news which potentially affect the Bitcoin volatility, all three of them are called via API.


Limitations

The forecast should be interpreted with several limitations in mind: 1. The forecasting model relies on historical market data.
2. External news and macroeconomic information are not directly incorporated into the prediction model.
3. Unexpected events may cause volatility to change rapidly.
4. Feature importance represents a feature's influence within the model and should not be interpreted as evidence of causation.
5. A high confidence score does not guarantee that the forecast will be accurate.
6. Financial markets are inherently uncertain, and historical relationships may not persist under future market conditions.
