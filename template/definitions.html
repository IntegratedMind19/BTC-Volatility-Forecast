Glossary


Confidence index: The confidence index measures the internal consistency of the forecast using indicators such as ensemble agreement and volatility persistence. A higher value indicates stronger agreement among the system's internal indicators. It is not a probability of accuracy and does not guarantee that the forecast will be correct.

Feature importance: Feature importance indicates the relative influence of each input feature within the trained Random Forest model. A higher importance value means that the feature contributed more to the model's decision-making across its trees. Feature importance does not establish causation and does not show whether a feature increased or decreased an individual forecast.
